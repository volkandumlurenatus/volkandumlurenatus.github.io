Health Companion Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

What the app stores

Everything you enter (profiles, health records, medications, cycle data, payment reminders and settings) is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.

Google Drive

If you turn on partner sync and link your Google account, the app uses the Google Drive permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With it the app can only create, read, update and delete the single file it created itself (healthcompanion-sync.hcb). It cannot see any other file in your Drive. Before upload the file is encrypted on your phone with AES-256-GCM using a password that you and your partner choose; Google and the developer cannot read its content. The file is shared by link so your partner's phone can download it; without the password the downloaded file cannot be opened.

What the developer receives

Nothing. The app has no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer never receives, sells or shares your data. Data from Google APIs is used only to provide the sync feature to you and is not transferred to anyone else.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes all data on the phone. In the app, Settings > "Tum verileri sifirla" deletes all records, and the sync screen button "Drive'daki dosyami sil" deletes the file from your Google Drive. You can also remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen of the app.
