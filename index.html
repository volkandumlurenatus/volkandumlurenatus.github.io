Health Companion

A personal Android app for two partners.

Health Companion is a private Android app to keep personal health and household records on the phone: toilet visits, weight and body measurements, calories, medications with reminders, the menstrual cycle calendar and payment reminders (credit cards, rent, bills).

Two partners can optionally follow each other's records. For this, each phone stores one encrypted file in the user's own Google Drive. The app asks only for the drive.file permission, which lets it see only the files it created itself. The file is encrypted on the phone with a password that only the two partners know (AES-256).

The app is not on Google Play. It has no ads, no accounts of its own and no servers.
